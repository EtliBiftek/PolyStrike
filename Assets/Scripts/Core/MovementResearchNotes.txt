PolyStrike movement tuning reference

Current CS2 values used as the starting point:
- sv_accelerate: 5.5
- sv_airaccelerate: 12
- sv_air_max_wishspeed: 30 u/s
- sv_friction: 5.2
- sv_stopspeed: 80 u/s
- sv_gravity: 800 u/s^2
- walk multiplier: 0.52
- crouch multiplier: 0.34
- crouch transition: 0.15625 s
- standing hull: 72 u
- crouched hull: 54 u

1 Unity metre is treated as 39.37 Source units.

Weapon benchmarks currently used for tuning are based on the AK-47 and M4A4 movement/accuracy envelopes. The game keeps its own weapon names and can diverge later for balance.
